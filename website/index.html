How it works

Three steps, in any app.

If an app has a text field, Hello can type in it: email, chat, notes, documents, your code editor and browser pages.

  1. ⌥
    1

    Hold Option

    Hold the shortcut (Option by default) in any app. The indicator shows that Hello is listening.

  2. 2

    Speak naturally

    Talk the way you normally do: Bangla, English, or both in the same sentence.

  3. Done
    3

    Let go

    Hello cleans up the text and pastes it where your cursor is.

Bangla + English

Talk the way you actually talk.

Most dictation tools make you pick one language. Hello cuts your speech into short pieces of about 1.5 to 3 seconds, works out the language of each piece, and sends it to the right speech model. Then it joins the text back together in order.

  • Switch mid-sentence. English with Bangla phrases, or Bangla with English words, in one dictation.
  • Word-level checks. Bangla words inside an English stretch are found with word timings and the Mac's spell checker, then transcribed again with the Bangla model.
  • You choose. Auto-detect, English only, or Bangla only. Turn on Bangla + English in Settings → Dictation → Languages.
  • Help tune it. “Help tune Bangla” is off by default. When you turn it on, Hello saves up to 100 dictations on your Mac, to tune detection to real speech. Nothing is uploaded.

Mixed speech is still being tuned, so results will vary. The examples on the right show how the pieces fit together. They are not a benchmark.

Notetaker

Meeting notes that know who said what.

When a call starts, Hello offers to take notes. If you say yes, it transcribes the meeting live on your Mac. No bot joins the call.

  • Works with your calls. Common meeting apps, calls in browser tabs, and chat-app calls and huddles.
  • Who spoke. An on-device voice model tells speakers apart. Name someone once and Hello recognizes their voice in later meetings. You can turn this off.
  • Where and when. Platform, app, browser profile and meeting link. With calendar access (off by default), the matching event's title and attendees too.
  • Everything in one place. Transcript, Overview, Notes and Summary tabs. Copy as Markdown, or move a meeting to the Trash.

Privacy

Your voice never leaves your Mac.

Audio is turned into text on your Mac, and nothing is uploaded. There are no accounts, no tracking and no cloud AI. Hello goes online only to download the speech models you choose and to check for updates.

On your MacSpeech, language detection and speaker recognition all run locally.
No accountsNo sign-up, no email, no API keys. Open it and talk.
No analyticsThe app has no analytics or telemetry.
Off until you turn it onCalendar access and Help tune Bangla are off by default. Meeting voice recognition can be turned off.

Requirements

Made for Mac.

Hello is a native Mac app. It is for Macs with Apple silicon, and it is not available for other computers or phones.

  • Apple siliconM1 or newer

Getting started

Ready in two minutes.

  1. Download Hello, open Hello.dmg, and drag Hello into Applications.
  2. Open Hello from Applications. The first time, macOS may ask you to confirm. See opening Hello the first time.
  3. Grant permissions when asked: Microphone to hear you, Input Monitoring to see the Option key, and Accessibility to paste text. Calendar is optional.
  4. Download a speech model from the setup screen.
  5. Click into any text field, hold ⌥ Option, speak, and let go.
  6. For Bangla: open Settings → Dictation → Languages, turn on Bangla + English, and download the Bangla model.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is Hello really free?

Yes. Hello is free to download and use, and it is released under the MIT license. There are no paid plans, no trials and no ads.

Is Hello open source?

Yes. All of Hello's code, every release and this website are on GitHub. You can read how it works, build it yourself, report a problem or suggest a change.

Why does macOS warn me the first time I open Hello?

Hello is signed with its own certificate, but it is not notarized by Apple, so macOS asks you to confirm once. Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway next to Hello. Updates install from inside Hello, so you normally only need to do this once.

How do updates work?

Hello checks for a new version once a day. When one is ready, Hello shows what's new. Confirm, and it downloads, installs and reopens itself. You can also check at any time from the menu bar icon → Check for Updates, or in Settings → General → About.

Does it work without internet?

Yes. After you download the speech models you need, dictation and meeting notes work offline.

Does it work on Windows, phones or Intel Macs?

Not right now. Hello is for Macs with Apple silicon (M1 or newer) running macOS 15 or later.

Which apps can I dictate into?

Any app with a text field: email, chat, notes, documents, your code editor, and browser pages. Hello pastes the text where your cursor is.

How well does it handle Bangla?

Hello has a speech model fine-tuned for Bangla, and it can switch between Bangla and English inside one dictation. Mixed speech is still being tuned. If you want to help, turn on “Help tune Bangla”. It keeps recordings on your Mac only.

Does a bot join my meetings?

No. Notetaker records and transcribes on your Mac, and nothing joins the call. The notes prompt also reminds you to let others know you're recording.

Where is my data stored?

Settings, history, models and meeting notes are kept in the voice-control folder inside ~/Library/Application Support. You can delete any dictation or meeting from inside Hello.
